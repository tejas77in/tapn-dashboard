Not a forecast -- current net liq plus each currently-open position's expected move to month-end. A position that actually expires by then uses its full POP-weighted outcome (POP x profit target, weighted against (1-POP) x max loss); a position with more DTE than days left in the month can't resolve by month-end, so it's modeled on theta decay instead, with best/worst case scaled to how much of its remaining life falls within this window. Does not assume any NEW trades between now and month-end; recalculates automatically as positions close and new ones open.
